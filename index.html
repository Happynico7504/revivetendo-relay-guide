Why Choose Revivetendo?

Bringing dead services back to life with a focus on simplicity and community.

Seamless Switching

Our custom Pretendo plugin allows you to switch between Pretendo Network and Revivetendo on the fly.

Community Driven

We host a custom Juxtaposition instance dedicated to active Miiverse communities, alongside regular matchmaking events.

Easy Setup

Just download the files, copy them to your SD card, and activate Revivetendo through the HB App. No complicated configurations required.

Supported Services

Enjoy fully functional online multiplayer and social features for these titles.

Make live video calls on your GamePad screen with full camera and mic support.

Play online matches with friends and others globally. Fully supports matchmaking.

Share handwritten messages and doodles with your friends online.

Custom Juxtaposition

Explore dedicated Miiverse communities. Share posts, drawings, and screenshots directly from your console.

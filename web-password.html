Web Password

Sign in to Revivetendo in a browser with your PNID and a web password.

What is the web password for?

It lets you sign in to Juxtaposition (Revivetendo) and to the dashboard from any browser. It is separate from your PNID password, which never leaves your console.

How to set your web password

  1. Connect your Wii U or 3DS to Revivetendo at least once (see the Guide).
  2. On the console, open Miiverse (Juxtaposition).
  3. Open your own profile and select Settings.
  4. Select Web Access, enter your new web password and save it.

Signing in

Use your PNID as the user name and your web password as the password. You can change the password at any time in the same place on your console.

Forgot it or locked out?

After 3 wrong passwords within 5 minutes, web sign-in for your account is locked for 15 minutes.

  • If your PNID is linked to Discord (/link_pnid on our Discord server), /unlock_web_login unlocks it right away.
  • /reset_web_password gives you a new web password, or set a new one on your console under Settings → Web Access.

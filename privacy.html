Privacy Policy

Information regarding the data we process and store on the Revivetendo Relay Network.

How Revivetendo Connects

Just like official servers, our network needs to verify your console to let you play online. When you connect, your console automatically sends its serial number, device ID/certificate, and a password hash to log in.

Why does my console send this?

This information is strictly technical and is only used to make your connection work! Because the Revivetendo Relay Network allows your homebrewed console to connect to custom services, we have to authenticate your device exactly the same way the official servers do. It helps us keep the network secure and stable for everyone.

What exactly is needed to log in?

  • Console Serial Number: Helps the network recognize your specific console when you connect.
  • Device ID / Certificate: A standard requirement for secure communication so your console can safely talk to the network.
  • Password Hash: Used to securely log you in. Your console automatically scrambles your password into a secure "hash" before connecting, so your actual password never leaves your console.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely! This technical data is never sold or shared. It simply exists to keep your console securely logged in and ensure the network stays up and running for everyone.
